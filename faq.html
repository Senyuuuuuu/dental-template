🦷 Dental Implants & Restorations

In almost all cases, yes. At Dentiva Dental Clinic, we use 3D CBCT bone mapping to evaluate bone density down to 0.1mm. If bone volume is deficient, we perform biocompatible synthetic bone grafting or sinus lifts concurrently with implant placement, avoiding secondary surgical sessions.

A single implant fixture takes approximately 30 to 45 minutes to place in-chair under local anesthesia. The titanium implant then integrates with your jawbone (osseointegration) over 8 to 12 weeks. Once healed, your custom monolithic zirconia crown is permanently torqued onto the abutment.

Our clinical documentation reflects a 99.4% integration success rate. We utilize grade-4 surgical titanium fixtures manufactured by Straumann (Switzerland) and Nobel Biocare (Sweden), backed by a comprehensive lifetime manufacturer warranty.

✨ Cosmetic Veneers & Laser Whitening

No. Dentiva specializes in minimally invasive "micro-prep" feldspathic ceramic veneers. We only contour 0.2mm to 0.3mm of the outermost enamel layer—equivalent to the thickness of a contact lens. We never aggressively file healthy teeth down to pegs.

Dentiva's proprietary cold-light laser utilizes photothermal filtration that operates at ambient oral temperature, preventing pulp chamber overheating. Additionally, our bleaching gel contains potassium nitrate and amorphous calcium phosphate (ACP) to seal enamel dentinal tubules, resulting in virtually zero sensitivity.

💳 Financing, Insurance & Sedation

We offer interest-free monthly financing for up to 12 and 24 months through our regulated healthcare credit partners (such as Chrysalis Finance). Applications take less than 3 minutes online with an instant soft credit check that does not impact your credit score.

We offer three levels of anxiety management: (1) Computerized Wand painless micro-numbing, (2) Inhalation sedation (nitrous oxide / happy air), and (3) Certified Consultant IV twilight conscious sedation, allowing you to relax comfortably throughout the procedure with zero memory of the dental work.
